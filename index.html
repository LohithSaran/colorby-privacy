Colorby Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Colorby app ("the App") handles your information. In short, the App does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal details.

Data stored on your device

To save your coloring progress, the App stores your artwork and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the App removes this data.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Network access

The App works fully offline and does not send your data over the internet.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the date above.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: support@wonderdoodle.in
